# Sportz Village Fundamental Skill Themes

Source: Sportz Village, *Creating Champions. In Life.* (March 2025), page 16, "Fundamental Skill Themes
that form the basis for Sports". In the book this is a table printed as an image, so its text cannot be
read from the PDF; this file carries it into the planner's knowledge base.

The fifteen skill themes underpin the sports in the EduSports programme. Units in the planner combine
several themes and never centre on one named sport; the plans use the props and space the teacher has.

1. Walking, Running
2. Chasing, Fleeing, Dodging
3. Jumping, Landing
4. Balancing
5. Transferring Weight
6. Rolling
7. Kicking
8. Punting
9. Throwing
10. Catching
11. Volleying
12. Dribbling
13. Striking with Rackets
14. Striking with Bats
15. Striking with Hockey Stick

Grade emphasis (EduSports pedagogy v1.0): Grades 1–2 (Early Movers) focus on fundamental movement skills
and movement concepts — themes 1 to 6 in particular. Grades 3–5 (Sports Focus) apply manipulative skills —
themes 7 to 15 — in modified team games, alongside cooperative games, fitness and yoga.
