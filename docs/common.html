EduSports Digital

Common safety & teacher notes

Applies to every EduSports Digital lesson plan. Lesson-specific safety points are on the plan itself.

Teacher's kit

  • Clipboard with class list, safe-travelling checklist and notes sheet
  • First-aid kit and class medical information (e.g. asthma inhalers, allergies)
  • Optional: 1 school device for assessment photos, only if school policy allows

Before the lesson

  • Check the space and equipment for hazards
  • Know which students have medical needs and where their medication is
  • Set water and first-aid points near the exit

During the lesson

  • Keep every student in your line of sight
  • Water breaks every 10–15 minutes; more in hot weather
  • Use a clear stop signal (e.g. whistle + "freeze") and practise it in the warm-up

Safeguarding

  • Any 1:1 support happens in view of the group
  • Physical contact only for safety (e.g. spotting), explained first, in full view
  • Never use exercise, exclusion or public comparison as punishment
  • No photos or video except for assessment under school policy; never in changing areas

Teacher notes

  • Group students inclusively; follow the class profile and school policy
  • Respect cultural and religious dress and modesty requirements
  • Record incidents and near-misses on the notes sheet the same day
